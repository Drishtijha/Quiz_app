## 🌷 Welcome to My Little Quiz Garden

**Quizly** is a sweet little quiz website created for curious minds who love learning in a fun and interactive way. From testing your knowledge to discovering new things, every quiz is a tiny step toward becoming smarter. ✨

Think of it as your own magical study corner — where knowledge meets creativity and every correct answer feels like a little victory! 🦋

## 👑 Enter the Princess Quiz World

- 🎀 **Interactive Quizzes** — Make learning fun, one question at a time.
- 🌸 **Choose Your Topic** — Explore subjects and challenge your knowledge.
- ✨ **Instant Feedback** — See how you perform as you answer.
- 🩷 **Track Your Progress** — Celebrate your learning journey.
- 🦄 **Simple & Friendly Design** — A lovely experience that makes studying feel lighter.
- 📱 **Responsive Experience** — Enjoy your quiz on different screen sizes.

*Features may vary depending on the current implementation of the website.*
